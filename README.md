# CrumbControl

A more thoughtful way to bake.

CrumbControl is a baking companion built for people who want greater consistency, sharper timing, and a better understanding of what makes each loaf successful. It helps bakers plan with intention, respond to the dough in real time, and learn from every bake.

## The idea

Baking is both an art and a science. CrumbControl brings those two together by helping bakers track the details that matter most: ingredient balance, fermentation timing, dough behavior, and final loaf quality.

Instead of relying only on memory or intuition, bakers can work with a clearer picture of what is happening in the dough and what to expect next.

## Built for better bread

### Smarter dough planning

CrumbControl helps bakers understand how ingredients affect the final dough before the bake even begins. It supports more informed decisions around hydration, structure, and ingredient balance, making the process feel more deliberate and less reactive.

### More confident fermentation timing

From bulk fermentation to shaping and cold retardation, the app helps bakers work with a clearer sense of timing. It supports better decisions at the moments that matter most, especially when dough is changing quickly.

### Real-time bake awareness

A bake can live across devices, with updates that keep the process connected as the dough evolves. Bakers can stay aligned on timing, folding, and dough behavior without losing momentum in the kitchen.

### Better learning after the bake

Each loaf tells a story. CrumbControl helps capture the details of the process and connect them to the final outcome, making it easier to learn from successes and improve on challenges.

## Why bakers use it

- To plan with more confidence
- To stay aware of dough changes in real time
- To keep the process organized across devices
- To understand what influenced the final loaf
- To build better habits with every bake

## A better baking rhythm

CrumbControl is designed to help bakers move from guessing to understanding. It supports a more thoughtful rhythm in the kitchen, where observation, timing, and refinement work together to create more consistent and more satisfying bread.
